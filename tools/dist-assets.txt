Game files to copy
==================

This package does not include the game's levels, graphics, music or sound.
Copy them from your own installed games into the folders below, which are
beside the wonderland program. Copy the files only; do not rename them.


assets/RTWP (required)
----------------------

From your Return To Wonderland folder:

    res1.dat to res10.dat
    res12.dat to res68.dat      there is no res11.dat

That is 67 files. The game stops at startup and names any that are missing.

Also copy these if you have them:

    options.cfg                 your settings
    players                     folder, your player profiles and progress
    CustomLevels                folder, custom levels
    CustomHouses                folder, for custom levels
    CustomModels                folder, for custom levels


assets/TOW (optional)
---------------------

From your Wonderland folder (The Original Wonderland). Without these, the
Original Levels and The Playground menu lines are not available.

    res1.dat to res22.dat
    bonus1.lev to bonus5.lev    the bonus levels, if you have them


When you are done
-----------------

    wonderland(.exe)
    assets/
        RTWP/
            res1.dat
            res2.dat
            ...
        TOW/
            res1.dat
            res2.dat
            ...

Settings and player files are saved in assets/RTWP, so that folder must be
writable.
